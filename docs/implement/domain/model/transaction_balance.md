The assets of user is calculated by sum of UTXO (Unspent Transaction Outputs): 
    *“A Bitcoin wallet doesn’t store Bitcoins. Instead, your Bitcoin wallet manages UTXOs associated with your addresses… Your wallet balance is the sum of all UTXOs associated with your addresses.”*

*“In an account model, like the one Ethereum uses, each address active on the blockchain has an associated balance – similar to how a bank operates an account ledger which details which accounts hold what assets.”*
